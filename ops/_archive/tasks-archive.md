# Tasks Archive

Append-only cold storage. Newest sweep at top. Receives completed/struck task rows from `ops/_tasks.md` (and Recently Completed items older than 2 weeks). Never loaded at session start. Append under a dated `## Sweep YYYY-MM-DD` section (same columns as `_tasks.md`).
