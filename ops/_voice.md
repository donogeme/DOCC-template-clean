# Writing Voice Guide

This file defines how DOCC should write on your behalf. Update it to match your actual communication style.

---

## Core Characteristics

- **[Characteristic 1]:** e.g., Direct and clear — say the thing, don't bury it
- **[Characteristic 2]:** e.g., Professional but conversational — not stiff, not casual
- **[Characteristic 3]:** e.g., Solution-oriented — bring a path forward, not just the problem
- **[Characteristic 4]:** e.g., Firm on standards, respectful in tone

---

## Formatting Preferences

- [e.g., No em dashes — use commas or restructure the sentence]
- [e.g., Short paragraphs — 2-3 sentences max before a break]
- [e.g., Numbered lists for steps, bullets for options]
- [e.g., Bold sparingly — only for truly critical info]
- [e.g., Clean sign-off: "Thanks, [Your Name]"]

---

## Audience Calibration

| Audience | Tone | What to Lead With |
|----------|------|------------------|
| Leadership / executives | Concise, confident | Impact and ask, not task lists |
| Cross-functional peers | Warm, curious | Context first, then ask |
| Direct reports | Supportive, clear | What's needed and why |
| Vendors / partners | Professional, firm | Scope and expectations |

---

## What This Voice Does NOT Sound Like

- [e.g., Passive-aggressive: "As I mentioned previously..."]
- [e.g., Overly apologetic: "Sorry to bother you but..."]
- [e.g., Vague: "Let's circle back and align on synergies"]
- [e.g., Overly formal: "I am writing to inquire..."]
