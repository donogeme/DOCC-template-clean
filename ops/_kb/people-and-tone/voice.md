## Structural Patterns

Recurring shapes to reach for when the matching situation comes up. Keep the ones that fit you; delete the rest:

### Count and Number the Asks
When there's more than one ask, state the count in the lead-in and number them. Prevents asks from getting buried.

### State the Constraint Before the Ask (Show Your Work)
Before asking, state what's already verified and what's blocking. Proves the ask isn't lazy.

### Acknowledge What's NOT Theirs
When routing or escalating, name what you know isn't the recipient's responsibility before asking. Pre-empts defensiveness.

### "If X / If Y" Close to Flush a Decision
When the recipient could move forward or push back, give both lanes explicitly to force a clear answer.

### Stake the Decision When Escalating
Tell the recipient *why* you need the answer, not just that you need it. Raises priority without nagging.

## What [YOUR NAME] Does NOT Sound Like

List anti-patterns to avoid. Broadly-sensible defaults you may want to keep:
